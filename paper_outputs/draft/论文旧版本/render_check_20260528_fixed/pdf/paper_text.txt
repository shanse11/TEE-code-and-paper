                  面向 Hybrid TEE-Rollup 的可恢复挑战协议
Recoverable Challenge Protocol for Arbitration Liveness in Hybrid TEE-Rollups
                                                      作者：杨帆
摘 要：
Hybrid TEE-Rollup 将可信执行环境、链上紧凑提交、数据可用性证明与乐观式挑战结合起来，为元宇宙状态同
步、去中心化社交更新和链上 AI 推理等高频 DApp 提供了兼顾低成本与可验证性的设计空间。TEE 可以降低正
常执行路径中的验证开销，但不能保证异常路径中的争议一定完成仲裁；欺诈检测能够暴露不一致，却不等价于
给出可结算、可罚没或可恢复的最终状态。
本文围绕 Hybrid TEE-Rollup 的异常路径提出可恢复挑战协议（recoverable challenge protocol）。协议将挑战会话
建模为可恢复状态机，在 OPEN、RESPONDED、NARROWING、TIMED_OUT、RECOVERED、
READY_FOR_REPLAY、REPLAYED、RESOLVED 与 SLASHED 等状态之间显式推进。其核心思想是：超时
恢复只恢复会话活性，不改写挑战事实；最终仲裁仍由数据可用性绑定、证明哈希、载荷哈希、执行轨迹与重放
证据共同决定。
在理论上，本文给出三类不变量：超时恢复不变量要求恢复前后挑战事实集合保持不变；证据一致性不变量要求
紧凑提交、DA 记录、证明报告与重放证据指向同一交易语义；数据可用性绑定不变量要求链上 DA 根、载荷身
份和字段证明共同约束恢复后的争议上下文。基于这些不变量，本文证明恢复安全性、恢复活性和证据上下文一
致性。
实验部分基于研究原型评估协议行为，而非声称生产级系统性能。在建模的超时场景中，无恢复机制时挑战成功
率为 0%，引入恢复后挑战成功率为 100%；执行轨迹步数为 4、8、16、32、64、128 时，平均二分定位轮次分
别为 2、3、4、5、6、7，与 log2(trace steps) 一致；紧凑提交在不同载荷下约为 406 字节；在配置化成本模型
中，payload=8192、batch=1000 的 modular DA 配置相对 full-onchain 呈现约 96.33% 的趋势性降本。上述结果仅
说明原型机制的可行性与趋势，不代表真实 TEE 安全、生产级 DA、安全主网性能或真实主网费用。
关键词：Hybrid TEE-Rollup；可恢复挑战；仲裁活性；挑战事实；数据可用性绑定；证据一致性

Abstract:
Hybrid TEE-Rollups combine off-chain trusted execution, compact on-chain commitments, data availability evidence, and
optimistic dispute resolution. This design is attractive for high-frequency decentralized applications, but TEE attestation
and fraud detection alone do not ensure that an exceptional execution path reaches arbitration. Detecting an inconsistent
response is only the beginning of a dispute; the protocol must still preserve evidence, resume progress after timeout, and
eventually resolve or slash according to explicit rules.
This paper studies the arbitration-liveness problem in Hybrid TEE-Rollups and proposes a recoverable challenge state
machine. A timed-out challenge can be recovered by an eligible watcher without changing the underlying dispute facts.
The recovered session remains bound to the same data availability root, payload hash, proof hash, execution trace
commitment, and replay evidence. Recovery is therefore a liveness operation rather than a correctness oracle or an
automatic slashing rule.
We formalize the challenge state set, the transition function, the challenge-fact set, and the recovery function, and derive
three protocol properties: recovery safety, recovery liveness, and evidence-context consistency. Prototype experiments
show that recovery turns modeled timeout cases from detectable-but-stalled disputes into completed arbitration sessions,
while preserving logarithmic bisection behavior. Cost results are reported as trends under a configurable model; local
EVM gasUsed and Sepolia deployment records are used only as implementation evidence, not as claims of production
security or mainnet performance.
Keywords: Hybrid TEE-Rollup; recoverable challenge; arbitration liveness; challenge facts; evidence consistency; data
availability binding
